# Comprehensive SEO Audit Report: Baloch Money Transfer

## 1. Executive Summary & Critical Issues
*The initial priority is to address critical security and indexing issues before moving to content generation.*

- **Suspicious Links:** Outbound links to suspicious sites (e.g., `123movies-to.org`, `embedgooglemap.net`) were found in the currency transfer page footer. This must be removed immediately.
- **Improper H1 Structure:** H1 tags on the homepage and internal pages are not structured correctly.
- **Outdated Content:** Old blog posts and news articles need updating.
- **Missing Dedicated Pages:** There are no optimized service pages for each destination country.
- **Technical SEO Needs:** Requires a full technical SEO review using Google Search Console.

## 2. Indexing & Technical Health Check

## 4. Image SEO
The website contains images related to the logo, office, licenses (AUSTRAC), money transfer methods, and identity verification steps.

- **Alt Text:** Must be written descriptively for all images.
- **Filename:** File names should be descriptive.
- **Optimization:** Images must be compressed and served in `WebP` format.
- **Lazy Loading:** Apply to images below the fold.
- **Dimensions:** Define `Width` and `Height` to prevent CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift).
*(Example naming convention: `baloch-money-transfer-sydney.webp`)*

## 5. Content SEO & Trust Signals
*For a financial business, trust is paramount. The following should be added to the site:*

- **Company Legal Info:** Registration number, valid licenses.
- **Office Address & Contact Info:** Clearly visible.
- **How it Works:** Explanation of the money transfer process, steps, fees, and conditions.
- **FAQ Section:** Create an FAQ to answer user questions directly.
- **Internal Linking:** Improve internal linking between related articles and service pages.
